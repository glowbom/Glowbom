# Glowbom

Turn your ideas into software. Create projects, work with AI agents, and try what you build.

Start with **Glowbom Mobile** on iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro, connected to Glowbom Desktop on your Mac.

**[Get started with Glowbom Mobile →](https://glowbom.com/mobile/)**

[Documentation](https://glowbom.com/docs/) · [Report a bug or suggest a feature](https://github.com/glowbom/Glowbom/issues/new/choose) · [Open-source code](https://github.com/glowbom/glowbom-oss)

This repository is the public home for Glowbom Labs. It hosts product feedback, [small apps](https://github.com/glowbom/Glowbom/tree/master/apps), and [earlier work](https://github.com/glowbom/Glowbom/tree/master/archive). [Karaoke](https://github.com/glowbom/Glowbom/tree/master/apps/karaoke) is the current app example.

## Start on your device

1. [Get Glowbom Mobile](https://glowbom.com/mobile/) for iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro through TestFlight.
2. [Install Glowbom Desktop](https://glowbom.com/desktop/early-access/) on your Mac.
3. [Pair your device](https://glowbom.com/docs/companion/), then open or create a project.

Keep Desktop open and your Mac awake on the same Wi-Fi as your device.

Follow builds, preview your app, and read your project notes in Project Book. With [Glowbom Tasks](https://glowbom.com/docs/tasks/), you can send tasks and messages to your agents, share files, and review their work from your device.

## Explore Glowbom

| Product | Where to start |
| --- | --- |
| **Mobile and Vision Pro** | [Work with your Mac projects from your device](https://glowbom.com/mobile/). |
| **Desktop for Mac** | [Create projects and work with your local coding agents](https://glowbom.com/desktop/early-access/). |
| **Web** | [Draw an idea in your browser](https://glowbom.com/draw/). |
| **OSS** | [Run the open backend, CLI, and Agent + Buzz browser interface locally](https://github.com/glowbom/glowbom-oss). |

Glowbom OSS works without Desktop or a Glowbom account for local coding. Desktop and the companion apps are separate products; their full interfaces are not included in the OSS repository.

## Bugs, questions, and ideas

This is the shared issue tracker for Glowbom. You can report problems or suggest improvements for Mobile, Vision Pro, Desktop, Web, OSS, and the apps in this repository.

[Browse existing issues](https://github.com/glowbom/Glowbom/issues) or [open a new issue](https://github.com/glowbom/Glowbom/issues/new/choose). Choose the product and include its version and your device when you can. Reports are public, so leave out passwords, API keys, pairing codes, and private account details.
